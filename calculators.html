Why lifespan is the number to be suspicious of

Every other input here is something you can look up. Lifespan is a projection, and it is where optimism enters the model. If your company is 8 months old, you cannot know your 20-month lifespan — you assumed it. Try halving it and see whether the verdict survives. If it doesn't, your business case rests on a guess.

The shortcut worth memorising

Break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ contribution margin. At a 50% margin you need 2×. At 33% you need 3×. At 25% you need 4×. This is why "we hit a 3× ROAS" means nothing until someone says what the margin is — the same 3× is excellent for a software company and a slow bleed for a reseller.

4 · A/B test significance

A two-proportion z-test — the standard check for whether a difference in conversion rates is real or noise. Enter the totals for each variant at the end of the test.

Three ways this test gets abused
  1. Peeking. Checking daily and stopping the moment it goes green massively inflates your false-positive rate. Decide the sample size first (use the planner below), then look once.
  2. Running less than a full week. Tuesday buyers and Sunday buyers are different people. Always run in whole weeks.
  3. Testing many things at once. Test twenty variants at 95% confidence and roughly one will look like a winner purely by chance.

The uncomfortable implication

Required sample grows roughly with the square of how small an effect you want to detect. Halving the detectable lift roughly quadruples the traffic needed. For most small sites this means honest A/B testing of small changes is simply out of reach — and the right response is to make bigger, bolder changes and judge them on reasoning and qualitative evidence, not to run underpowered tests and pretend the results mean something.

Step by step

    A closing caution on all six

    Every one of these is a model, and a model is an argument made of assumptions. Their real value is not the number they produce — it's what happens when you change one input and watch the verdict flip. That tells you which assumption your plan actually depends on, which is the thing worth going and checking.
